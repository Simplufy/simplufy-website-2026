# Transactional keyword SERP analysis for Simplufy

Research date: 2026-05-28

Method: collected the first 8 organic-style results via DuckDuckGo HTML for the 20 priority transactional keyword clusters, then fetched/analyzed each ranking page for page type, headings, content depth, CTA/proof signals, and likely ranking factors. A few pages blocked automated fetching; those are marked as unfetched but still counted as SERP results.

Important: use this as strategic SERP intelligence, not a guaranteed Google ranking report. Before publishing final pages, validate the exact live Google SERP in your target geography.

## Executive SEO pattern

- Generic service queries are dominated by authoritative listicles/guides and SaaS/product pages. To compete, Simplufy should build hybrid service pages: commercial intent first, then guide-level depth, FAQs, examples, and proof.

- Industry-specific queries are more winnable because many top pages are thin agency landing pages or generic guides. Simplufy has a proof advantage in auto detailing, ceramic coating, CRM/booking, and cross-brand automotive automation.

- The strongest content moat is proof: screenshots, numbers, campaign tables, CRM diagrams, before/after website examples, and industry-specific funnels. Most ranking pages explain the tactic; fewer show real implementation/results.

- Page templates should include: outcome-led hero, industry pain points, what we build, channel/process breakdown, proof/case studies, pricing factors, FAQ schema, internal links to service + industry pages, and a booking CTA.
